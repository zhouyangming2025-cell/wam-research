## B. Neutral mechanism record

### B1. Training graph

Metis uses a video-generation expert and a smaller action expert in a Mixture-of-Transformers. The attention mask allows future video tokens to attend to predicted future action tokens, while action tokens cannot attend to future video tokens:

```text
action tokens → future-video expert
future-video tokens → action tokens = masked
future-video loss → shared/action parameters through training gradients
```

The video target is a logged factual future video latent. The action expert predicts an action chunk with a flow-matching objective.

### B2. Deployment graph

```text
current observation + instruction/state
→ current-context representation
→ action expert flow denoising
→ trajectory/action chunk
```

Future-video tokens, future-video denoising, and explicit future video generation are bypassed. The exact source-level implementation is not verified because runnable code was unavailable.

## D. Evidence boundaries and unresolved items

- “Action→world” is a training-time forward dependency; it does not mean future video is read by the planner.
- The reported co-training gain does not isolate video-generation fidelity from representation shaping, model capacity, or multi-task regularization.
- Paper/README identify the paradigm, but exact code-level inference routing remains unavailable at the audited commit.
