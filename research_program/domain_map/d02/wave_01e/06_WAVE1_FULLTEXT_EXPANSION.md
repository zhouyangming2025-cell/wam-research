## Reading rule

The audit asks whether full text changes any of the following:

1. scope (`IN-DOMAIN`, `BOUNDARY`, or evidence-limited boundary);
2. final commitment topology (`R0`, `RH`, `RB`, `RJ`, `RG`, or `N/A`);
3. qualifying online world/consequence carrier (`W0–W5`);
4. resolver semantics (`E0`, `EF`, `EV`, `ED`, `EW`, or `EY`);
5. ordered learning/deployment fate.

Random samples, training labels, simulator inputs, and internal model states do
not become a route-level candidate resolver unless candidate identity survives to
a common resolver that determines the final committed output.

## Why P0057 is a real additional pressure case

The relevant text is explicit: the plan query first decodes future waypoints;
inference then optimizes those waypoints, and `τ*` is selected from
multiple-shooting trajectories by minimizing a cost containing an occupancy
collision term. This meets the four-part `PB` gate at the human layer:

```text
candidate birth → identity-preserving shooting set → common cost/optimizer → τ*
```

Therefore, the shallow `R0 + W4 + E0` row under-described the final commitment
topology. The corrected coarse route is `RB + W4 + ED`.

The predicted occupancy sequence is shared across ego candidates rather than
candidate-indexed. At the backend level the conservative projection is:

```text
C3@B(shared future occupancy)
· X=N/A
· D1
· P=PB
· V=VD
· T=(present, absent, present, absent)
· L=LS
```

This is legal at the backend level because the shared carrier conditions the
candidate-selection path; it is not a `D2` candidate-consequence form. It does,
however, expose a pressure against the current human compression rule that
`W4 + RB` normally requires `X3`. The same pressure already appeared in BeTop.
No rule is changed here.

## Preservation decision

The expanded pass confirms that the existing axes express the newly inspected
cases without adding paper-specific categories. The only unresolved issue is a
constraint-level question: whether a shared future-horizon carrier may feed a
candidate resolver without `X3` when the candidate identity exists only in the
ego planning branch. BeTop and UniAD now independently instantiate this case.

The evidence supports recording the pressure, not silently changing the rule.
