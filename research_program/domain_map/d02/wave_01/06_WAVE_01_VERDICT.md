## Required questions

### 1. Does the current route layer remain compact?

Yes. The 20 papers are covered by five deployed route families and three explicit boundary families. The map does not need another primary axis to describe direct future-conditioned planning, candidate resolution, semantic decision→action, joint world/action emission, or generation/simulation terminals.

### 2. Are route collisions informative?

Yes. `R0 + W4 + E0` groups direct future-conditioned planners while retaining carrier provenance as an evidence/modifier issue. `RB + W0` groups candidate resolvers but preserves `EV` versus `(EF+ED)`. `RH` and `RJ` remain visibly different from `R0`.

### 3. Are differences being pushed into the right layer?

Mostly. Architecture, representation, solver family, and number of samples remain implementation attributes. Learning order, target provenance, retain/drop fate, and resolver semantics remain in `L`, `E`, and audit attributes. The unresolved carrier-gate questions are not hidden; they are escalated.

### 4. Did any work require a new code?

No. Vista’s reward capability, GenAD’s joint generation, TOAD’s CEM, and VAD’s vectorized future motion all fit existing route semantics. Their unresolved parts are evidence or mode boundaries, not new dimensions.

### 5. Did any work expose a false collision?

No false collision was established. The audit explicitly refuses to collapse P0010 with P0021 at the full `E`/`L` level, refuses to collapse generation-only works with deployed planning, and keeps P0034 unresolved.

### 6. Did any work expose a false split?

No. Once solver and representation choices are ignored, the observed splits correspond to final commitment topology, online carrier role, resolver semantics, or ordered learning fate.

### 7. What must happen before expansion?

The next approved step would be evidence closure for the six escalated cases, especially P0004/P0058 carrier qualification, P0010/P0021 resolver semantics, P0029 joint-emission topology, P0038 terminal reward use, and P0034 source recovery. That step is not performed here.

### 8. Is Wave 2 started?

No. No Wave-2 selection, corpus expansion, ontology amendment, commit, or push was performed.

The current mechanism remains a compact route map rather than a return to dense paper-by-paper taxonomy. Wave 1 supports `PASS-EXPAND-EVIDENCE-LIMITED`, with the explicit condition that expansion continues to use one-row shallow records, evidence-first escalation, and no automatic ontology changes.
