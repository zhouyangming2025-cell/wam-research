## 3. Wave operating rules

1. The ordinary census record is one row per paper/artifact mode only when terminal output, deployment causal role, or lifecycle materially differs.
2. Selection is based only on local identifier, exact title, available abstract, year/venue/source metadata, code availability, and the declared research task. Method sections, formulas, mechanism diagrams, code paths, and existing projections are not selection criteria.
3. The final selected set is sealed in `01_SELECTION_LEDGER.md` before selected-paper method inspection.
4. Every selected paper receives one shallow record in `02_SHALLOW_MECHANISM_CENSUS.md`; no full ontology-free card is created.
5. The primary explanatory output is `R–W–E ⊕ L`. `C–X–D–P–V–T–L` is used only for escalation, collision, and boundary cases.
6. `UNKNOWN` is retained when evidence cannot close a field. It is not converted to absence or N/A.
7. Architecture, backbone, representation substrate, decoder depth, solver count, dataset choice, loss weight, and implementation naming remain audit attributes unless they change terminal causal topology.
8. Artifact splitting is allowed only for a real change in terminal output, action commitment, online world involvement, generation-only versus planning mode, paper/code causal path, or an inseparable training/deployment mechanism boundary.
9. No ontology edit, new code, new modifier, or automatic amendment is allowed. Any pressure is recorded as `POSSIBLE_STRUCTURAL_PRESSURE` or `STRUCTURAL_PRESSURE` in the wave verdict and held for a later boundary review.
10. Wave 2 is not started in this task. No commit or push is performed.

## 4. Coverage intent

The 20-paper wave is deliberately mixed to test:

- online world/future participation in planning;
- training-only predictive or representation transfer;
- candidate generation and scoring;
- world generation and simulation boundaries;
- joint or semantic world–action modelling;
- counterfactual, interactive, and closed-loop boundary cases;
- ordinary end-to-end planning systems that may be adjacent but not WAM.

The mix is for coverage, not quota-based preclassification. A paper may end as `BOUNDARY` or `EXCLUDE` after shallow inspection.
