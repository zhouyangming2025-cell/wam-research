## C. Reading use under field reconstruction

These papers should be read to understand:

- how interactive prediction/planning evolved before modern WAM terminology;
- what "reactive", "causal" and "counterfactual" mean operationally;
- how policy evaluation changes when world agents respond;
- which older concepts are being re-expressed with modern generative/latent world models;
- what current reactive simulators can and cannot validate.

P2-R-specific questions may still be recorded as secondary diagnostics, but no paper should be reduced to its effect on P2-R.
