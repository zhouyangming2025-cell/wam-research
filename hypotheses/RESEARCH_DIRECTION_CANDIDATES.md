# Research Direction Candidates

Status: exploration only. No method commitment.

## Candidate A — Risk-aware Latent World Model

Idea:

Learn future latent states containing both world evolution and risk evolution.

Potential value:

Connect existing risk-field expertise with modern WAM.

Open question:

Can risk representation improve planning beyond ordinary future prediction?

---

## Candidate B — World Model Uncertainty for Planning

Idea:

Use uncertainty of future prediction as a decision signal.

Open question:

Does uncertainty correspond to actual driving risk?

---

## Candidate C — Future Risk Representation Learning

Idea:

Predict future safety-related representations jointly with future world state.

Open question:

What supervision defines future risk?

---

## Candidate D — Counterfactual Safety World Model

Idea:

Evaluate alternative actions through safety-aware future prediction.

Open question:

How to distinguish true counterfactual prediction from candidate scoring?

---

Selection principle:

Do not choose by novelty alone. Choose by evidence, feasibility and planning relevance.
