# Risk-aware World Model Research Space

## Motivation

Current driving world models mainly focus on predicting future states, representations or trajectories. Safety and risk evolution are often implicit.

## Core Question

Can future risk evolution become an explicit component of a driving world model and improve planning reliability?

## Traditional Risk Field View

```
Environment
    ↓
Risk representation
    ↓
Decision
```

## World Model Extension

```
Observation
    ↓
World Model
    ↓
Future latent world
    ↓
Risk evolution representation
    ↓
Planning
```

## Research Questions

RQ1: Can risk be represented as a future world state?

RQ2: Can predicted risk evolution improve long-horizon planning?

RQ3: Can world model uncertainty serve as a safety signal?

## Status

Research hypothesis space only. Not yet a final method proposal.
